Mashriq Almuqaddimah Company for Contracting started as an MEP supply company in Saudi Arabia. We supply mechanical, electrical, plumbing, fire-fighting, and safety products to contractors and businesses.

As the company grows, Mashriq is expanding into other areas. New companies are being developed under the Mashriq name, including Rentals, Operation & Maintenance, and Facility Management.

Each business will operate as a separate division, with its own area of work and services.

Mashriq today is an MEP supply company, with plans to operate across several service sectors in the coming years.
